# Freeman’s Reach Systems Lab — Public Change Log

> A sanitized, event-driven record of meaningful public-facing project changes.

## Purpose

This file records selected changes that help readers understand how **Freeman’s Reach: Systems Universe** evolves as a systems-engineering project.

It may document:

- What changed
- Why the change was made
- Which engineering, research, or configuration-management concern triggered it
- Which public artifacts are affected
- What development area is currently moving forward

This is **not** a mirror of the private project Change Log.

The authoritative project records are maintained in the private Google Drive project repository. Public entries are deliberately sanitized and may omit unpublished canon, story spoilers, proprietary technical details, internal research issues, rejected concepts, working assumptions, AI handoffs, and other material not cleared for public release.

A public Change Log entry does **not** create or modify canon.

## How to Read This Log

Entries are **event-driven**, not artificially daily. A new entry is added when something meaningful changes or reaches a useful public milestone.

Common headings include:

- **Added** — a new public artifact, control, method, or repository capability
- **Changed** — a meaningful revision to existing public material or project architecture
- **Research** — a sanitized research milestone that materially affects development
- **Released** — a new public technical, academic, or systems-engineering artifact
- **Repository** — structural or governance changes to the public repository

Dates reflect the public project record and may not expose the exact timing of every underlying private research or canon decision.

### Changed

- Clarified the project architecture so the **Freeman’s Reach** series/universe title is no longer treated as an automatic in-universe civilization name.

- Verified that real-world astronomical discovery chronology must constrain in-universe naming and historical knowledge rather than being retroactively imposed on an earlier fictional population.

### Systems

- Expanded the internal system-domain roadmap beyond the initial technology set to ensure future development also covers medicine, defense/security, resource extraction, agriculture, sensing/navigation, logistics, and knowledge preservation.
- Continued to treat major capabilities as interdependent systems requiring requirements, interfaces, constraints, failure modes, and verification rather than as isolated narrative devices.

### Repository

- The public repository remains a sanitized, non-authoritative publication layer.
- Public transparency will increasingly document **how** the universe is engineered, researched, reviewed, and changed while private canon and source records remain under controlled project authority.
