# Human-Led, AI-Assisted Development Method

> A public, sanitized description of how **Freeman’s Reach: Systems Universe** uses artificial intelligence while preserving human creative authority, research traceability, and configuration control.

## Purpose

Freeman’s Reach is developed as a human-led systems-engineering and creative project. Artificial-intelligence tools are used to increase the capability of a bootstrap development team, but they do not hold project authority and their output does not automatically become fact, canon, or publication-ready material.

**Jason V. Holmes is the project creator, owner, and final canon authority.** He establishes the project’s goals, originating concepts, requirements, creative boundaries, research direction, architecture decisions, approvals, rejections, and final direction.

The private Google Drive project repository remains authoritative. This public document is informational only.

## What AI Is Used For

AI systems may support research organization, systems-engineering analysis, requirements development, architecture analysis, trade studies, continuity review, contradiction detection, failure-mode analysis, historical and technical research support, quantitative modeling, working drafts, editing, documentation, and independent cross-review.

AI output is treated as **provisional work product** until it is checked against evidence and project controls.

## Contribution Attribution

For meaningful development milestones, public records may distinguish four elements:

- **Owner Contribution** — what Jason originated, required, corrected, selected, rejected, or approved.
- **AI Assistance** — what AI systems researched, analyzed, drafted, checked, organized, or compared.
- **Evidence** — what external research, official sources, academic literature, or community feedback materially informed the work.
- **Result** — what public-facing architecture, methodology, research direction, or artifact changed.

This is intended to make the development process legible rather than to imply that every individual message or minor edit requires public attribution.

## Private Provenance Controls

The private project maintains additional provenance beyond the public repository, including controlled canon, decision, change, research, and work registers; source records; document version history; and a creator-contribution journal for significant owner inputs and reasoning.

Those private records are not copied wholesale to GitHub because they may contain unpublished canon, spoilers, working assumptions, rejected concepts, internal research issues, or private development conversations.

## Manuscript Boundary

Universe-development artifacts and publication-ready literary prose are different work products.

AI may assist with brainstorming, research, outlining, critique, continuity, editing, and provisional working text. Publication-ready manuscript work remains subject to deliberate human creative control and to the requirements of the applicable publisher, platform, contest, contract, copyright regime, or submission policy.

The project does **not** optimize text or records to defeat AI-detection systems. The preferred controls are truthful disclosure when required, clear human decision authority, source and version traceability, and documented development provenance.

## Canon and Configuration Control

A statement does not become fictional canon because it appears in an AI response, working draft, diagram, GitHub commit, public issue, or research note.

Canon follows a controlled process:

**Idea → Analysis → Proposed Canon → Approved Canon → Canon Register**

The public repository is a sanitized publication layer. Private Google Drive controls remain authoritative.

## Universe Architecture and Future Writers

The project is being designed so future authors and cross-media collaborators can work inside bounded, documented story sandboxes rather than depending on oral lore transfer.

A future writer assignment may specify an approved era, location, civilization or polity, available technologies, institutions, usable characters, character-creation permissions, protected canon, unresolved questions the writer may not answer, required continuity interfaces, and review requirements.

The planned writer-facing **Universe Architecture Matrix** is a derivative navigation layer. It links collaborators to approved rules and boundaries but does not create authority to fill blank or unresolved canon.

## Why This Is Public

The development process is part of the project’s systems-engineering case study. Public transparency is intended to show how a complex fictional universe is researched, constrained, reviewed, changed, and eventually made reusable across novels and other media.

The goal is not to claim that AI was absent. The goal is to make clear **how it was used, what the human creator contributed, and how final project authority is controlled**.

## Maintenance

This methodology may change as publishing practices, platform rules, copyright guidance, AI capabilities, collaboration structures, and the project’s own development process evolve. Material changes will be reflected through the repository’s public development records when appropriate.
